Can physiotherapy be provided online?

Many physiotherapy assessments and rehabilitation programmes can be delivered remotely. An initial consultation should establish whether online care is appropriate and whether in-person assessment or onward referral is needed.

Who is online coaching for?

Online coaching can work well for people who want structured support, accountability and a personalised programme without needing to attend a clinic for every session.

Do I need gym equipment?

Not necessarily. Your programme can be designed around the equipment you have available and your goals.

What if I need face-to-face care?

If remote care is not appropriate, you can be advised about seeking an in-person assessment or another appropriate healthcare professional.
